THRESHOLD

A cognitive agents visual novel
[[Begin|Intro Cinematic]]
In 2071, we stopped feeling alone. Unannounced and unprovokedly intrusive, or so it would seem. A structure several times bigger than the international space station appeared low in orbit, orbitally locking around the pacific ocean. Several small communities reported a strange unidentified flying object in the night sky, occasionally blowing up on social media and receiving various criticisms over their authenticity. The numerous governments and high standing organisations wasted no time however, containment of the exposure was in full force. The great war that happened a decade prior saw many people draw in the realisation that there might actually be some merit to numerous conspiracies around the elites hoarding the world's wealth. The world wasn't ready for a revelation as big as that was, so the information was classified. The following years weren't so kind for the existing institutions who have just started trying to survive, the knowledge of an alternate lifeform, worse yet the supposedly enemy governments somehow organising surprisingly well in hiding the fact could have been devastating for the public trust and cooperation. By 2079 practically everyone knew. Undoubtedly. What followed wasn't first contact. It was the first of the invisible collapse. People started questioning the current local orders, the very environment they lived in, the people they thought they held close, the distant new force in the sky. The global economic crisis of the upcoming decade protruded the sentiment even further, the sentiment that never got a call back, at least not from the right people. The fringe groups, all with their own ideologies offered solutions and promises that others wouldn't, the groups people were previously skeptical of, all started receiving more attention from all angles. Over the next 20 years, seventeen governments fell. The ones that didn't fell later, or were absorbed into something that called itself an order but functioned more like a pressure system, holding everything down so nothing could rise. A strong autocratic "federation" which represents the global order and the interests of all humans. In the huge gaps it left behind, other things grew. As they always do, but never before on this big of a scale. Criminal networks. Whole shadow governments. Organisations that don't have names you'd recognise but have reach you'd find impressive. Some of them made contact with the visitors. Not quite as diplomatically as commercially. It turns out the visitors were curious about us. Not enough to fully intrude, but enough for them to have stuck around for decades since. The year is 2137. The diplomatic, albeit not entirely documented relations between these unearthly visitors and the human collective have taken a turn that isn't entirely unexpected. Seeing the results and potential prospects of these trades, many groups took to trading various artifacts and trinkets from them, usually in exchange for some mundane items and biological samples. These samples, unbeknownst to a majority of the people, don't exactly exclude other humans. Some people were unfortunately, against their own will, caught up in the newly present mercantile system that runs outside of the usual framework, and found that out the hard way. You are about to be one of them.
